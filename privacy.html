Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

10THOU (“we”, “the app”) is a hardware reference tool and engineer community. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what control you have over it.

Information we collect

Most of 10THOU works without an account. When you choose to sign in — required only to post, reply, or claim a profile — we receive the following from LinkedIn through their OpenID Connect sign-in flow:

  • Your name (as shown on LinkedIn)
  • Your email address
  • Your profile photo (URL only)
  • A LinkedIn identifier (the sub claim) so we can recognize you on subsequent sign-ins

If you later add a job title or other profile details inside 10THOU, we store that too. Anything you post (photos, captions, thread titles, replies, spec tags) is stored so it can be displayed.

If you purchase Pro we receive a Stripe customer identifier from Stripe so we can record that your account is Pro. We do not store your payment card details — Stripe handles that directly.

How we use your information

  • To display your real name, title, and photo on your posts, replies, and profile so other engineers can identify you.
  • To let you sign back in without re-entering credentials.
  • To operate Pro features you have paid for.
  • To prevent abuse (e.g. rate limits keyed to your account).
What we don't do

We do not sell your data. We do not share LinkedIn-sourced data with advertisers or data brokers. We do not scrape public LinkedIn profiles — we only use what LinkedIn sends us through the sign-in you approve.

Third parties we use

  • LinkedIn — authentication (you authorize them to share name, email, photo with us). Governed by LinkedIn's privacy policy.
  • Supabase — hosted database and storage for your posts, replies, and profile. Governed by Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Vercel — application hosting. Governed by Vercel's privacy policy.
  • Stripe — payment processing for Pro. Governed by Stripe's privacy policy.

Your rights

  • Access — you can view all data on your own profile inside the app.
  • Correction — you can edit your title and other profile details.
  • Deletion — you can delete your account. Your user record, posts, threads, and replies will be permanently removed. To request deletion, email privacy@10thou.com.
  • Disconnection — you can sign out at any time from the profile button in the header.

Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. After deletion, data is removed from primary storage within 30 days. Anonymized, aggregated statistics (e.g. daily post counts) may be retained.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser local storage for your preferences (theme, saved lists, pinned specs) and for your authenticated session token if you sign in. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Children

10THOU is a professional tool not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the app or by email to signed-in users. The "Last updated" date above will change.

Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@10thou.com.
